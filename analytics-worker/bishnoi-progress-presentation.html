Admin side · Oct 8

Our own analytics platform

Built from scratch on Cloudflare Workers and a D1 database — no Google Analytics, no subscriptions, and no visitor IP addresses ever stored. The site now measures itself, privately.

What it records

  • Page views and client-side navigation
  • Every link and button click, classified

How it respects visitors

Admin side · Oct 9

Location accuracy upgrade

IP lookups place the connection, not the person — mobile carriers and VPNs often exit in another country. The platform now cross-checks every visit against the device's own time zone and records the country the visitor is really in.

How it works

  • The tracker sends the device's time zone (e.g. Europe/Rome) with every event
  • The Worker maps it to a country and compares with the IP's country
  • When they disagree, the device wins — misleading city data is dropped rather than shown
  • Verified against a real mobile session and the stored records aligned

Why it matters

Country data feeds the globe, the per-country stats and future market decisions. Accurate inputs mean the team can trust where demand is actually coming from.

Strategy

Familiar to buyers

Procurement teams navigate our site the way they navigate the market leaders — no learning curve between "found it" and "requested a quote."

Credibility by association

Matching the structure and polish of billion-dollar distributors signals that Bishnoi Omniverse operates at their standard, whatever its size today.

The right conversion model

B2B medical buying is quote-driven, not cart-driven. Benchmarking confirmed the quote list was the correct bet before a line of it was built.

SEO parity

Category and product URL structures mirror how the industry's search traffic actually flows, so pages compete on the terms buyers use.

A roadmap for free

Their public sites preview what buyers will expect next — account portals, order history, availability — so our roadmap is grounded in proven demand.

An edge they lack

We move faster: content updates ship same-day and the analytics loop shows within hours how buyers respond — agility the giants cannot match.
