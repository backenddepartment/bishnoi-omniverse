The audit · 19 October

Where the site stood

A search for our own domain on Google returned only unrelated "Bishnoi" pages. The site was built well underneath, but it was missing the signals that tell Google what each page is about and that the business can be trusted.

Working well

  • Pages fully readable by Google without running scripts
  • Fast responses, gzip and CDN caching
  • robots.txt correct, sitemap with 367 pages plus an image sitemap
  • Clean URLs like /medical-equipment/gloves/

Holding the site back

  • Not on Google, not even for the brand name
  • No canonical tags, no structured data, no share tags
  • Product descriptions 369–566 characters (Google shows ~155)
  • Homepage H1 had no keywords; no articles; thin product pages
  • No Search Console or Analytics

Part 1 · B1 · Critical

Canonical tags on every page

LiveEvery indexable page

The problem

No page told Google which address is the "official" one. The same page can be reached with or without a trailing slash, or with tracking links attached, so Google could split its ranking between duplicates.

What we did

Every page now names its own official address. Private pages (search results, quote list, 404) deliberately do not, so they never compete with real pages.

<!-- in the head of the gloves page --> <link rel="canonical" href="https://bishnoiomniverse.com/medical-equipment/gloves/">

Part 1 · B2 · Medium

Sitemap with real dates, robots.txt in place

Live368 pages · 79 images

The problem

Every page in the sitemap carried the same "last changed" date: the moment the site was rebuilt, which happens every hour. Google learns that dates like that mean nothing and ignores them.

What we did

Each page's date now comes from when its content actually last changed, read from the project history. Editing one product moves only that product's date. The deploy runs this automatically.

<url> <loc>https://bishnoiomniverse.com/catalog/</loc> <lastmod>2026-10-20</lastmod> <!-- real edit date --> </url>

Part 1 · B3 · B4 · High

Structured data: telling Google who we are

Live

The problem

Only breadcrumbs were marked up. Google had to guess who the company is, where it operates, what each of the 326 product pages sells, and which text on /faq/ is a question and answer.

What we did

Added schema.org data that Google reads directly, built from the site's own data so it always matches what visitors see.

Organization · homepage

  • Name, logo, description
  • Philippines and India office addresses
  • Phones, email, sales contact
  • Corp and LLP as sub-organizations

Product · 326 pages

  • Name, description, category
  • Product image where available
  • Official product address

No brand or price yet: the catalog has neither.

FAQPage · /faq/

  • Every question and answer on the page
  • Read from the same list the page shows, so they can never disagree

Two more fixes the audit did not list

Matching business details

Problem: the About page used the email contact@bishnoi.ai, and the footer (on every page) showed a different office address from the rest of the site. Google compares these with the Business Profile; mismatches weaken trust.

Fixed: one email and one address everywhere:

  • il@bishnoiomniverse.com
  • Unit 301 and 305, 17 Vatican Building, Vatican City Drive, B.F. Resort Village, Talon II, Las Piñas City, Metro Manila 1747

Desktop pages showing phone layouts

Problem: a styling setup bug, there since 8 October, made Global Network, Group Structure and Trade & Partners show their phone layouts on desktop, so pages were longer and harder to read.

Fixed: corrected the styling configuration. Desktop layouts apply again; phones are unchanged.

Part 2 · A1 · A2 · How it was done

Tell Google the site exists

A1 · Search Console (30 min)

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console, sign in with the company Google account
  2. Add property → Domain → bishnoiomniverse.com
  3. Copy the TXT record Google shows
  4. BigRock → Orders → bishnoiomniverse.com → DNS Records → TXT → leave Domain/Sub-domain empty, paste the value, choose a TTL, Save Record
  5. Back in Search Console click Verify (retry after 15–60 min if needed)
  6. Sitemaps → submit sitemap.xml and sitemap-images.xml

Needs: company Google account · BigRock login · ✓ done 24 Oct

Part 2 · Things to consider

What to keep in mind

Keep business details identical

Name, address, phone and email must match exactly across the website, Business Profile, LinkedIn and directories. Copy them from the website footer.

Do not change page addresses

Google ranks URLs. Renaming a category or product in the sheet changes its address and resets its ranking; tell the web team first so a redirect can be added.

Keep the product sheet clean

The catalog, sitemap and search descriptions are built from the Google Sheet every hour. Accurate names and descriptions there mean accurate pages on Google.

Hidden homepage keywords

The homepage H1 keywords are visually hidden. Low risk, but if Search Console ever flags the homepage, switch to a visible keyword line.

Claims need sign-off

Regulatory statements (FDA, CSP, certifications, delivery times) are read by Google and buyers alike. Every new claim goes through compliance before publishing.

Consistency beats intensity

Two good articles a month, steady link-building and monthly reviews will outperform a one-off burst. SEO is measured in months, not days.
